Everything measured so far — by us, and by everyone who has published on this — counts the papers that cite a retracted paper directly.

Those authors, at least, had the retracted paper in their hands. They could have checked.

This is the ring past that. Papers citing a paper that cited it. Those authors have no reason to know a retraction sits anywhere in their reference chain, because nothing tells them.

Watch the two columns move apart.

Direct citation does fall. From three hundred and seven a year, down to twelve.

Second-hand citation climbs. From two thousand nine hundred, to over nine thousand.

The year before the retraction, there were ten second-hand citations for every direct one. Now there are three hundred and ninety-six.

The correction reaches the first ring. It never reaches the second.
